what HTPC mode keeps. Decisions only; the work happens later.

Verdicts: **keep** (as is), **keep, edited** (small in-place edit),
**replace** (needs a `Tv*` version), **delete** (not needed in HTPC).
`pending:` = proposed, waiting for review.
